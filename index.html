Privacy Policy — Food Scanner AI

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Food Scanner AI ("the app", "we") handles information when you use the app. We built this app to be as privacy-respecting as possible: we do not require an account, we do not collect personal identifiers, and we do not run ads.

1. Information We Collect

  • Product photos: When you use "Scan by Image," the app sends the photo(s) you take or choose (front and/or back of a product) for AI-based analysis.
  • Barcode numbers: When you scan a barcode, the app sends that number to look up product information.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, or any account information, because the app does not have a login system.

2. How We Use This Information

  • Barcode lookups are sent to the Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, and Open Products Facts APIs (open, community-run product databases) to retrieve product details such as ingredients and nutrition facts.
  • Product photos are sent to our own backend server, which forwards them to Google's Gemini API for AI analysis, then returns the result to your device. Our backend does not permanently store the photos or the analysis results — they are processed and discarded.

3. Third-Party Services

Because the app relies on external services to function, some data passes through:

  • Open Food Facts / Open Beauty Facts / Open Products Facts — receives barcode numbers only.
  • Google (Gemini API) — receives product photos for the AI analysis feature only.

These third parties process data under their own privacy policies, linked above.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

Your Recent Scans and Favorites lists are stored locally on your device only, using standard app storage. They are never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage permanently deletes this data.

5. Permissions We Request

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and take product photos.
  • Photos/Gallery: to let you choose an existing photo instead of taking a new one.
  • Internet: required to look up products and run AI analysis.

6. Children's Privacy

Food Scanner AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Security

All network requests use encrypted connections (HTTPS). While no method of transmission is 100% secure, we take reasonable steps to protect the information that passes through the app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date above.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at red.elyou.devapp@gmail.com.
